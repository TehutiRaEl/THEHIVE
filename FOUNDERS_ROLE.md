# The Founder’s Role — Extrapolate and Turn

The founder does not need to build. The founder’s role is:

| Role        | Description                                                                 |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Extrapolate | See where KAI’s builds are heading and steer them toward the highest potential |
| Turn        | Convert KAI’s builds into revenue — license, sell, or scale them            |
| Approve     | Review KAI’s major decisions and provide constitutional oversight           |
| Plant       | Plant new seeds — new ventures, new visions, new possibilities              |

The founder is the Spore Planter.  
KAI is the Mycelium.  
Together they grow the Forest.
