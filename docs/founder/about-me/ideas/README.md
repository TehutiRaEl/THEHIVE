# About-Me / Ideas — The Intake Shelf

> **Plain words:** founder ideas that arrived as about-me material land here
> first. This is the door, not the house.

## What belongs here

An idea the founder shares about himself, or alongside something about
himself — a "what if we…" that came with a story, a memory, a wish, or a piece
of who he is. One idea per file, named plainly, with three lines at the top:
what it is, where it came from (his words, date), what it would change.

## The rule that matters

**Every idea here is copied into the pool — `docs/founder/ideas/` — the one
place Kai El gathers from.** This shelf feeds the pool. Nothing lives only on
the shelf. If an idea is only here and not in the pool, the filing is not
finished.

## What does NOT belong here

- Plans (an idea with steps, order, owners) → `../plans/`
- Updates (news from the founder) → `../updates/`
- Vision (direction for the whole Hive) → `docs/FOUNDERS_VISION.md`
- Decisions → `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`
- Anyone else's ideas. This folder is the founder's only.

## Rules

- An idea here is not approved work. Nothing is built from this shelf.
- Never invent an idea and file it as the founder's.
- A dropped idea is marked dropped with the date and the founder's reason, and
  stays as a record. Ideas are never silently deleted.
