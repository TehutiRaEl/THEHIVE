# About-Me / Plans

> **Plain words:** an idea with steps, order, and owners — "here is how we…".
> A plan is not approved work until the founder says yes.

## What belongs here

Founder plans: an idea that grew steps — what happens first, what happens
next, who does what, what "done" looks like. One plan per file, named plainly,
dated, with the idea it came from named at the top (link to the pool file in
`../../ideas/` where there is one).

## What does NOT belong here

- Raw ideas (no steps yet) → `../ideas/` (then the pool at `../../ideas/`).
- Updates → `../updates/`.
- Vision → `docs/FOUNDERS_VISION.md`.
- Decisions → `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`.
- The Queen batches (BATCH-Q*) and the 20-rung ladder — those live in their
  own places in the repo, not here. A founder plan may point to them, but does
  not replace them.

## Rules

- A plan here is not approved work. It becomes work only when the founder
  says yes to that one plan, one question at a time — and it still waits its
  turn, TO Queen 100%.
- If a plan is decided, note the decision: link the `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`
  entry or the merged ratification PR. A chat yes alone is not a decision.
- If a plan is dropped or held, mark it so with the date and the founder's
  reason — "no" means "not yet", a hold, never a fold.
- Never build straight from a plan in this folder. The plan is the map; the
  founder's go-ahead is the permission to walk it.
