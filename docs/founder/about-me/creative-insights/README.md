# About-Me / Creative Insights

> **Plain words:** how the founder sees, makes, and imagines things — the
> creative side of who he is, kept where the Hive can learn from it.

## What belongs here

Founder creative insights: a way of seeing a problem, a story seed, a world
rule for The Atherian Universe, a lens on how something should feel, a
"here is how I think about this" that is creative rather than a plan or a
decision. One insight per file, named plainly, dated, with the founder's
words kept as his words and any agent summary marked as a summary.

## What does NOT belong here

- Finished lore or canon for the books — that lives in the TAU repo / SOURCES
  intake, not here. A creative insight here may point to it, but does not
  replace it.
- Ideas meant for the pool → `../../ideas/` (via `../ideas/`).
- Plans → `../plans/`.
- Drafts of unfinished work → `../drafts/`.
- Updates → `../updates/`.

## Rules

- Never silently overwrite or "improve" the founder's creative words. If
  something contradicts earlier lore or an earlier insight, flag it for the
  founder's ruling — do not smooth it over.
- Knowledge is free to learn from, but never reproduce copyrighted words or
  claim trademarked words. Summarize in the Hive's own words.
- An insight is not a decision and not approved work.
