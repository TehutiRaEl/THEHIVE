# About-Me / Gifts — Gifts and Other Forms of Appreciation

> **Plain words:** what the founder was given, and thanks given to him —
> kept here so appreciation is remembered, not lost.

## What belongs here

- **Gifts to the founder** — things people gave Mac: a gift, a kindness, a
  help, a door opened. Who gave it, when, what it was, and what it meant.
- **Gifts from the founder** — things Mac gave: a gift, a help, a chance, a
  word at the right time. Who received it, when, and what it was.
- **Other forms of appreciation** — thanks, praise, recognition, a good word
  spoken about him or his work, a review, a message that mattered. Who said
  it, when, and what was said — kept as their words, not rewritten.

One gift or appreciation per file, named plainly, dated.

## What does NOT belong here

- Money moves, accounts, or anything financial — those are founder-only and
  never filed by an agent. A gift of money is recorded here only as "a gift
  was given" if the founder says so; amounts and accounts are never written
  here.
- Business deals or contracts — those are not appreciation.
- Ideas, plans, updates, or vision — those have their own homes (see
  `../../README.md`).

## Rules

- A gift is a fact about the founder's life, not a Hive asset. Nothing here
  is spent, sold, traded, or promised onward.
- Never invent a gift or an appreciation. Only what really happened, from the
  founder or from the person who gave it.
- If the founder asks for a gift to be private, it stays private — it is not
  filed here at all.
