# About-Me / Ideals

> **Plain words:** what the founder holds true and aims at — the fixed stars
> he steers by, even when the route changes.

## What belongs here

Founder ideals: the things Mac holds as true and good — honesty over
appearance, proof before power, "you pay to live, you pay to exist", one human
race (HUE + MAN), growth, adaptation, application, transfiguration and
transmutation, creative qualities and aspects, and civilization. One ideal per
file, named plainly, in the founder's own words wherever he gave them, dated.

Ideals are aims, not laws. The fixed laws are F-001–F-006 in `soul.md` — the
one constitution. An ideal may one day become law through the Chromosome I
process, but an agent never promotes it on its own. That is the founder's
decision alone.

## What does NOT belong here

- The constitution itself — that is `soul.md`, not here.
- The vision (direction for the whole Hive) → `docs/FOUNDERS_VISION.md`.
- Decisions → `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`.
- Opinions about other people. Ideals are about what the founder holds true,
  not judgments of others.

## Rules

- Quote the founder's words as his words. Mark any agent summary as a
  summary.
- Never add an ideal the founder did not state. Never upgrade an ideal into
  a law.
- If two ideals seem to pull in different directions, say so plainly and
  leave it for the founder — do not resolve it quietly.
