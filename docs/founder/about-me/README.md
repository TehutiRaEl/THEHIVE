# About the Founder — The About-Me Directory

> **Plain words:** this folder tells the Hive about the founder — who Mac is,
> how he thinks, what he values, what he made, what he was given, and what he
> is working on. Any agent that works for him reads here first, so it does not
> guess about him.

## What lives here

| Subdirectory | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| [`WHAT_MUSE_THINKS_ABOUT_MAC.md`](WHAT_MUSE_THINKS_ABOUT_MAC.md) | What Muse thinks about Mac — the five-reason read of why he is building the Hive, plus the vision he gave himself, plus the alignment synthesis. Muse's read, in plain ELI5 words. Open to Mac's correction. |
| [`ideas/`](ideas/) | Founder ideas that arrived as about-me material — the intake shelf that feeds the pool at `../ideas/`. |
| [`creative-insights/`](creative-insights/) | Founder creative insights — how he sees, makes, and imagines things. |
| [`gifts/`](gifts/) | Founder gifts and other forms of appreciation — what he was given, and thanks given to him. |
| [`ideals/`](ideals/) | Founder ideals — what he holds true and aims at. |
| [`drafts/`](drafts/) | Founder drafts — unfinished work, not yet decided, not yet published. |
| [`plans/`](plans/) | Founder plans — ideas with steps, order, and owners. A plan is not approved work until the founder says yes. |
| [`updates/`](updates/) | Founder updates — news from the founder, dated, newest first. |

## The automatic-update rule

Anything the founder adds to his about-me gets sorted into the right
subdirectory here, immediately and automatically — ideas to `ideas/`,
creative insights to `creative-insights/`, gifts and appreciation to `gifts/`,
ideals to `ideals/`, drafts to `drafts/`, plans to `plans/`, updates to
`updates/`, and anything else founder about-me related to a new subdirectory
named plainly for what it holds, with its own short README. Sorting is filing,
not deciding. Filing never approves, publishes, or spends anything.

## The separation rule (short version)

Ideas are not plans. Plans are not updates. Updates are not vision. Vision is
not about-me. Vision lives only in `docs/FOUNDERS_VISION.md`; open questions
live only in `docs/FOUNDER_QUEUE`; answered questions live only in
`docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`. The full rule is in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## What does NOT belong here

- The constitution (`soul.md`). Nothing here changes it.
- Open founder questions — those go to `docs/FOUNDER_QUEUE`, not here.
- Decided questions — those go to `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`, not here.
- The vision — that lives in `docs/FOUNDERS_VISION.md`, not here.
- Anyone else's about-me. This folder is about the founder only.
