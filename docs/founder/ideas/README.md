# The Founder's Ideas — The Pool

> **Plain words:** this is the jar where ideas go so they are not lost and not
> forgotten. Ideas live here. Ideas are NOT built all at once.

## What belongs here

Any idea from the founder — a "what if we…", a spark, a direction worth
keeping, a thing to try one day. One idea per file, named plainly for what it
is (for example `faceless-esoteric-shorts.md`), with three lines at the top:

1. **What it is** — the idea in one or two plain sentences.
2. **Where it came from** — founder's words, date, and where he said it.
3. **What it would change** — what would be different if it happened.

Ideas that arrive through the founder's about-me land first in
`../about-me/ideas/` (the intake shelf) and are then copied here. This folder
is the one pool. There is only one pool, so Kai El always knows where to look.

## What does NOT belong here

- **Plans.** An idea with steps, order, and owners is a plan — it goes to
  `../about-me/plans/`.
- **Updates.** News from the founder goes to `../about-me/updates/`.
- **Vision.** Direction for the whole Hive lives only in
  `docs/FOUNDERS_VISION.md`.
- **Decisions.** A decided question lives in `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`. An idea
  here is not a decision.

## How Kai El uses this pool

Kai El gathers from here when he needs an idea — he picks from a multitude
instead of being ambiguous about his own. Gathering is not building. Nothing
here is built until the founder says yes to that one thing, one question at a
time, and nothing is built before its turn — TO Queen 100% first.

## How an idea leaves this folder

An idea leaves only in one of these ways:

1. The founder picks it — it becomes a plan (`../about-me/plans/`), then a
   question in `docs/FOUNDER_QUEUE`, then a decision in
   `docs/FOUNDER_ANSWERED`.
2. The founder drops it — the file is marked dropped with the date and the
   founder's reason, and stays as a record. Ideas are never silently deleted.
3. It stays — gathering, waiting for its turn.

## Rules

- Never build straight from this folder. No idea here is approved work.
- Never invent an idea and file it as the founder's. Only the founder's ideas
  live here; anything else is labelled as whose it is.
- Never let the pool go stale on purpose — but never force an idea into a
  build to clear the pool, either. A full pool is the point: a multitude to
  gather from.
