## What this means for the Queen plan (findings, not scope changes)

The single most important correction: **the Q-plan builds Nanuet as an operator who delegates work through Akosha — but every source that defines her says she does not command, does not execute, and does not delegate; she remembers, governs by memory, and vetoes by constitution. The operating/delegating job belongs to Kai El** (chief-of-staff/operator, 060; "finalize Kai El first," 050), with Akosha as his dispatcher. The Q-plan's "Nanuet owns the campaign queue, observe→advise→delegate" (confirmed by Mac, Q1, 2026-11-05) needs the division written into BATCH-QC before tier 3 is built: she governs the queue (what is in it, what matters, whether it aligns, what it produced); he operates it (breaks directives into jobs, assigns via Akosha). Without that sentence, tier 3 builds the wrong agent doing the wrong job — and "delegate" appears in zero HIVE_UPDATES files, so the shape has no dated source behind it at all.

Second: the identity question (Nanuet vs Queen vs THEHIVE) must be answered by Mac before BATCH-QZ can certify "the Queen is 100%" — the plan cannot verify a subject its own sources define three different ways.

Third: Q2's risk table already has a dated founder decision behind it — the 2026-08-10 Kai El tiers (050). Recording that as the table's provenance, and labelling the buckets Kai El's (set under Nanuet's governance), closes Q2 without inventing anything.
