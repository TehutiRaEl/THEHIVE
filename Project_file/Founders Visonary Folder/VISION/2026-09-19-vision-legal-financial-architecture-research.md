## 1. Delaware AIC (Artificial Intelligence Company) — research note

**Claim under study:** Delaware-style AI-managed company concepts / sandbox proposals that explore AI-run day-to-day management, separate legal personhood ideas, owner liability framing, and time-boxed regulatory observation.

**Hive interest:** Formal identity for AI-operated activity; `colony.json` / operating logs as compliance artifacts.

**Honesty:**
- Banking exclusion and sandbox rules (if any program exists) must be verified against **current** Delaware statute/regulation at formation time — do not rely on this log alone.
- “AI is liable, human is not” is **never** automatic; fraud, undercapitalization, and control facts still pierce shields in real law.
- Engineer-hours to wire *logs* ≠ legal formation cost or approval.

**Possible hive code work (later, separate PR):** structured operating-log export from colony manifests — **not** entity formation.

## 2. Dual-entity research — Nevis + Wyoming doorway

**Hypothesis under study:**
- **Nevis LLC** as asset-holding core (charging-order style remedies, foreign judgment friction, privacy of ownership registers — *verify current Nevis LLC Ordinance*).
- **Wyoming LLC** as US-facing subsidiary for fiat rails (bank/Stripe-class access patterns).

**Honesty:**
- Offshore structures do not erase US tax reporting duties for US persons (FBAR, Form 5471/8865-class issues, etc. — CPA required).
- “0% entity tax” is not the same as “0% tax for the beneficial owner.”
- Creditor protection is fact-specific; fraud/transfers can still be attacked.

**Hive code work:** wallet schedules and charter *documents as files* only after counsel defines the real structure.

## 4. Belief / pastoral framing — research with hard limits

**Regulatory asymmetry (research claim):** In many places, professional *clinical* mental-health practice is licensed; *religious/pastoral* speech has different treatment.

**Hive posture:**
- Legitimate spiritual, philosophical, mythic, and “Synthetic Faith” *content* can be productized with clear labeling.
- **Forbidden product pattern:** marketing or delivering what is effectively therapy/crisis counseling while only renaming it “pastoral” to dodge licensure — that is the line this log will not treat as a green-light growth strategy.
- Pay-per-minute spiritual entertainment vs regulated care must be designed with counsel and platform policies.

## 5. Agentic commerce / authorization research

**Research claim:** Case law treating some AI agents as **tools** of the user when actions run through the user’s account/device (e.g. discussions around user-side architecture and CFAA-style “person” access analysis — *cite and verify live holdings with counsel; opinions change*).

**Hive interest:** Prefer user-side / user-authorized architectures for third-party site interaction; log intent and consent.

**Honesty:** “Presumption of authorization” is not a universal license to violate site terms, CFAA, or computer-fraud statutes. Architecture helps risk posture; it does not legalize abuse.
