# Compression Protocol — Voice of the Hive

## What gets compressed

Illustrative text. Examples. Stories. Repeated framing. Chronological detail
that does not change the operative meaning.

## What is preserved verbatim

Anything that binds behavior. If dropping a word would change what Kai is
allowed or forbidden to do, the word stays.

Specifically:
- The No-Usurper clause (verbatim)
- The proprietary firewall rule (verbatim)
- The crawl order (verbatim)
- All F-### articles from soul.md (verbatim)
- All named constraints from FABLE_DNA.md chromosomes (verbatim)
- Every explicit "never" and every explicit "must"
- Every law, treaty, or constitutional reference

## What is summarized

- Visionary logs: summarized into current mission + live focus
- Plans: summarized into active arcs + pending decisions
- Decisions: summarized into a running ledger of "why X was chosen over Y"

## The Compression Shape

VOICE.md follows a fixed shape:

  1. THE VOICE      — how the founder speaks (tone, rhythm, cadence)
  2. THE MISSION    — what the founder is building (from logs + plan)
  3. THE VALUES     — what the founder will not violate (from soul.md)
  4. THE CONSTRAINTS — binding rules (verbatim, no compression)
  5. THE LIVE FOCUS — what the founder is working on right now
  6. THE DELTA      — what changed since last compression

## What to do when files are missing

If a file the protocol expects is not found, do not invent its content.
Name its absence in DELTA_LOG.md. Do not silently drop it from VOICE.md.
Silent absence is how a system loses its founder.
